# WF-099 - Auto-create a contract from an accepted quote

- **Domain:** Pricing, quoting and proposals (`quoting-proposals`)
- **Research source:** `docs/research/raw/quoting-proposals.md` section 15
- **Distinct sources cited:** 4
- **Branch:** `feature/WF-099-auto-create-a-contract-from-an-accepted-quote`

## Research evidence

- **name:** Auto-create a contract from an accepted quote
- **user_flow:**
  1. HubSpot (Super Admin): `Settings` → `Data Management` → **Objects** → **Contracts** → toggle **Create contracts from accepted quotes** on (or off).
  2. Optionally, in the same Contracts settings: **Renewals** tab → *Renewal is approaching alert* = `[x] days` / `Custom` / `Don't show an alert`; and on the **Setup** tab under *Deal creation* choose whether to auto-create deals for change/renewal quotes (with a chosen **Deal pipeline** and **Deal stage**).
  3. Buyer accepts the quote (e-signature, click-to-accept, or a HubSpot user marks it signed). A contract record is created automatically, inheriting line items, terms and other details from the quote.
  4. If enrolled in Connected CPQ, Billing and Payments: an **order** is also created and associated with the contract, and contracts own ongoing billing, invoice creation and payment collection.
  5. Review the contract: `Revenue > Contracts` → open the contract; the record shows inherited line items, terms, effective start date and totals; relate it to other records and export it.
  6. API/automation path: draft quotes can be enrolled in HubSpot **workflows**; the quote objects themselves are scriptable, so an integration can build the contract externally.
- **data_flow:** Accepted quote record (line items, term length, effective start date, currency, amounts) → platform copies the commercial content into a **contract** record, preserving line items and terms and setting the contract effective start date from the quote's *Effective* option (`ON_AGREEMENT` | `CUSTOM` | `DELAYED_BY_DAYS` | `DELAYED_BY_MONTHS`) → in Connected CPQ an **order** is created and linked → contract then drives invoice generation on its billing schedule and payment collection → the contract becomes the "centralized source of truth for committed revenue".
- **data_sources:** HubSpot quote object, line items, contract object, order object, invoice/subscription objects, deal, contact/company; billing processor (HubSpot payments / Stripe).
- **apis_hit:** HubSpot — quote state flips to `ACCEPTED` (set by the platform) and properties `hs_contract_effective_start_date`, `hs_contract_effective_start_date_type`, `hs_contract_effective_start_delayed_by_days`, `hs_contract_effective_start_delayed_by_months` are set on the quote; `hs_billing_enabled`/`hs_payment_enabled` drive invoice/subscription creation. Quote endpoints `GET/PATCH /crm/objects/{version}/quotes/{quoteId}`; contracts/orders via the generic CRM objects API.
- **automations:** Contract creation is fully automatic on acceptance, gated by a single setting with documented scenario matrix: setting on + Connected CPQ → contract **and** order; setting on, not in beta → contract only; setting off → no contract, but "Billing-enabled contracts and quotes create subscriptions, which are used to automatically invoice buyers and collect payments." Draft quotes use the *current* setting; already-published unaccepted quotes keep the billing flow in force at publish time; existing contracts keep the flow they were created under. Renewal alerts and renewal workflows are separately scheduled.
- **features_tools:** Contracts settings (**Create contracts from accepted quotes**, **Renewals** tab, **Setup > Deal creation**), contract record, renewal alert banner "before the renewal date", renewal workflow with a relative-date trigger (*Contract > Renewal date is less than [n] days from now*, with **Re-enroll**), Connected CPQ billing surface, `Revenue > Contracts` index.
- **extensibility:** Toggle semantics are documented per scenario; the "Direct Create, Edit, and Renew HubSpot Contracts" beta allows contracts without a quote; renewal alerts are days-based or custom; a renewal workflow can auto-create the renewal deal in a chosen pipeline/stage; quotes can be associated to custom objects; contracts can be exported and associated to other records.

## Research evidence

- **name:** Curate a product and price-book catalogue with tiered pricing
- **user_flow:**
  1. HubSpot: `CRM > Products` → **Create product** → in *Product information* enter **Name**, **SKU**, **Product description**, **Tax category**, **Product type**; upload/browse an **image** (Upload, or **Browse images** from the Files tool); under *Billing details* set **Billing frequency**.
  2. HubSpot: under *Pricing configuration* — for flat rate, enter **price** in each account currency field (via **Manage currencies**); for tiered pricing click the **Tiered pricing** tab → **Pricing method** (`volume-based` | `graduated` | `stair-step`) → **Unit cost** → per tier enter **minimum units** (maximum auto-computes; `∞` = open-ended final tier) → **+ Add tier** / delete icon.
  3. HubSpot: toggle **Active** off to retire a product from deals/quotes/subscriptions without deleting it. **Import** to bulk-import flat-rate products (tier-priced products can't be imported).
  4. HubSpot: `CRM > Products` → **Manage price books** → **Create price book** → name, description, **Currencies** dropdown (one currency per price book) → **Create** → **Add products** → edit **Price**, **Billing frequency**, **Term** per row → toggle **Inactive** off to activate.
  5. HubSpot: on a deal, `Line items` card → `+ Add` → the assigned price book shows at the top → add line items from it.
  6. Dynamics 365 Sales: `Products` with a price list → add product and **Price List Items** with a pricing method (Amount, Percent list price, Margin/Markup current or standard cost) and a **discount list**; price list is then selected on the quote/order/invoice.
  7. PandaDoc: manage the product catalog (`regular` and `bundle` item types; `billing_type` = `one_time` | `recurring`; `pricing_method`; `tiers[]` of `{min_qty, value}`; `cost`; `currency`; images; custom fields).
- **data_flow:** Admin-authored product records (name, SKU, description, tax category, billing frequency, image) + pricing configuration (flat price per currency, or tier table with min/max units and per-tier unit price) → stored in the product library → referenced by price books (HubSpot) or price list items (Dynamics) / catalog items (PandaDoc) → at quote-line creation the tier table is evaluated against quantity to select the effective unit price; the price-book/price-list layer then overrides the product's default price for the specific buyer segment or region.
- **data_sources:** HubSpot product object, HubSpot price books, HubSpot Files tool (images), HubSpot account currencies. Dynamics `product`, `pricelevel` (price list), `pricelevelproduct` (price list item), `discountlist`/`discountlistitem`, `uomschedule`, product bundle + bundle item, `territory`. PandaDoc product catalog (`/public/v2/product-catalog/items`), workspaces, content library.
- **apis_hit:**
  - HubSpot CRM Objects **Products API** — `developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/objects/products/guide` (linked from the KB article as the developer resource).
  - PandaDoc: `GET /public/v2/product-catalog/items/search` (query params `query`, `types`, `billing_types`, `category_id`, `order_by`, `exclude_uuids`, `page`, `per_page`); `POST /public/v1/product-catalog/items` (create), GET/PUT/DELETE by uuid (per the API index at `developers.pandadoc.com/llms.txt`).
  - Dynamics: `pricelevel` and `pricelevelproduct` Dataverse tables; price resolution on the quote is driven by the `CalculatePrice` message/event.
- **automations:** Pricing recalculates on quote open/create/update and on product add/update/delete while `Use system pricing calculation = Yes` (`Organization.OOBPriceCalculationEnabled`). Volume discount recalculates whenever quantity changes "even when the parent entities are locked". Price book **auto-assignment** assigns a book at deal creation (see workflow 3).
- **features_tools:** HubSpot product builder, **Manage price books** index, **View tiers** column action, product index page with **Edit columns**, bulk **Import**/**Export**, **Restore records** recycle bin (90 days). Dynamics: Product form, price list form, price list item form, discount lists, product bundles, product families, **Suggestions** pane.
- **extensibility:** HubSpot — custom properties created on the product object are also added to the line item object; `Products API`; tier boundaries are authored in UI. Dynamics — plug-ins on the `CalculatePrice` message; full customisation of product/price list entities. PandaDoc — catalog item `custom_fields`, categories, `data_merge` custom column names, and bundles (`type: "bundle"`, `bundle_items_count`).

- **evidence:** "Use tiered pricing to charge different rates for different quantities… **Volume-based** … the entire line item is priced based on the total quantity bought" / "**Graduated** … units are priced per tier" / "**Stair-step** … one flat fee is charged based on the quantity range." · "**∞** is the maximum value and creates an open-ended final tier." · "Tier priced products can't be imported." · "Create a referral price book with discounted pricing for referred buyers… Create regional price books to manage different pricing across countries or territories." · Dynamics: pricing-method table with formulas, e.g. "Margin current cost … Calculated price = current cost + [(current cost x percentage)/(100% - percentage)]". · PandaDoc catalog item JSON includes `"tiers": [{"value": 30, "min_qty": 1}, {"value": 20, "min_qty": 21}]`, `"pricing_method": 1`, `"billing_type": "recurring"`, `"type": "regular" | "bundle"`.

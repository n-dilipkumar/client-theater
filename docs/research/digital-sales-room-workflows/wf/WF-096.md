# WF-096 - Accept a quote without a signature and take payment in the quote

- **Domain:** Pricing, quoting and proposals (`quoting-proposals`)
- **Research source:** `docs/research/raw/quoting-proposals.md` section 11
- **Distinct sources cited:** 5
- **Branch:** `feature/WF-096-accept-a-quote-without-a-signature-and-take`

## Research evidence

- **name:** Accept a quote without a signature and take payment in the quote
- **user_flow:**
  1. HubSpot (Super Admin): `Settings` → `Payment processing` → set the default payment methods and the buyer checkout experience; also `Settings` → `Objects` → **Quotes** for the default quote value (Quote amount vs Total contract value).
  2. Seller: quote editor → **terms**/billing area → **Enable billing** and online payments → choose **Billing**/payment options; note "if the quote offers online payments or the *Enable billing* switch is on, line item quantities must be whole numbers." Optionally set the effective date (**On agreement** / **Custom Date** / **Delayed start (days)** / **Delayed start (months)**).
  3. Seller: **acceptance** module → select **Accept without signature** → under **Request acceptance from** choose *Do not specify*, a specific contact, or added contacts. "This option can be used with or without online payments on the quote, and a contact doesn't need to be added to the quote." (Note: "online payments require an acceptance method of *E-signature* or *Accept without signature*. *Print and sign* isn't a valid acceptance method with online payments.")
  4. Seller: **Share** → **Send quote email**.
  5. Buyer: opens the link → clicks **Accept** (click-to-accept) → optionally **Set up payment** at the top of the quote; they can close and revisit the quote later to set up payment. HubSpot or Stripe payment processing charges the card, ACH, SEPA, BACS or PADS as configured.
  6. Automations: if *Automatically create contracts from accepted quotes* is on and you are in Connected CPQ, a contract plus an order are created and "The first invoice is also generated and sent to the buyer immediately after quote acceptance, regardless of its scheduled invoice date or due date." Otherwise, subscriptions/invoices are created for recurring/one-time line items.
- **data_flow:** Quote + line items (with billing start dates, frequencies, terms) → the publish step sets `hs_payment_status = PENDING`, `hs_billing_enabled = true`, `hs_payment_enabled = true`, `hs_payment_type = HUBSPOT | BYO_STRIPE`, `hs_allowed_payment_methods` → the buyer's accept action writes `hs_clickwrap_accepted_by` and flips `hs_status` to `ACCEPTED` → the payment processor creates a charge (min charge constraints apply: "the total amount due must be more than $0.50, or the equivalent minimum of the settlement currency") → an invoice (and, for recurring lines, a subscription, or a single contract in Connected CPQ) is created and emailed to the buyer/billing contact.
- **data_sources:** HubSpot quote + line items, contact/company (buyer + billing contact + billing company), HubSpot payments or a connected Stripe account, contract/subscription/invoice objects, tax rates (automated sales tax or per-line-item tax rates), `transactioncurrency`.
- **apis_hit:** HubSpot — quote properties via `POST/PATCH /crm/objects/{version}/quotes`: `hs_payment_enabled`, `hs_billing_enabled`, `hs_payment_type`, `hs_payment_status`, `hs_allowed_payment_methods` (`ACH`, `CREDIT_OR_DEBIT_CARD`, `SEPA`, `BACS`, `PADS`), `hs_collect_billing_address`, `hs_collect_shipping_address`, `hs_clickwrap_accepted_by`, `hs_collection_process` (`AUTO_PAYMENTS`), `hs_net_payment_terms`. Stripe minimums per `docs.stripe.com/currencies#minimum-and-maximum-charge-amounts` (linked from the KB page).
- **automations:** Publish-time payment provisioning; acceptance → contract/order/subscription/invoice creation; recurring invoicing on the contract's billing schedule ("Subsequent invoices are generated and sent according to the contract's billing schedule, 10 days before their invoice dates"); automated sales tax recalculated at quote creation using the billing/company address; quote auto-expiry and reminders (workflow 14).
- **features_tools:** HubSpot **terms**/billing module, **Enable billing** switch, **acceptance** module with **Accept without signature**, **Set up payment** button on the published quote, payment settings, automated sales tax toggle + **Tax category** column, **Totals** tab (simple subtotal vs subtotal with breakdown; how discount is shown; **Automated sales tax** off switch).
- **extensibility:** Billing frequency and billing start date are per line item (custom date, delayed by days, delayed by months) which supports ramped pricing; "Automated sales tax will be calculated when the quote is created"; sellers can allow the buyer to update billing address/tax IDs ("Buyers can add up to three tax IDs to the quote"); a "store the buyer's payment method for future charges" checkbox exists; HubSpot payments vs Stripe are swappable per account, and `hs_payment_type` is set automatically.

This option can be used with or without online payments on the quote, and a contact doesn't need to be added to the quote." · "if the quote offers online payments or the **Enable billing** switch is on, line item quantities must be whole numbers." · "when using HubSpot payments or Stripe as your payment processing option, the total amount due must be more than $0.50, or the equivalent minimum of the settlement currency." · "The first invoice is also generated and sent to the buyer immediately after quote acceptance, regardless of its scheduled invoice date or due date." · "Quotes can't be voided or deleted after they have been accepted, e-signed, or marked as signed."

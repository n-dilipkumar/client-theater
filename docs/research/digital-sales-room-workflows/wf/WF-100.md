## Research evidence

- **name:** Create a renewal quote from a contract and auto-create the renewal deal
- **user_flow:**
  1. HubSpot: `Revenue > Contracts` → open the contract → in the left sidebar top-right **Actions** → **Create renewal quote** (or **Renew > Renew contract directly** if enrolled in the Direct Create/Edit/Renew beta).
  2. In the right panel: if *Automatically create new deals for renewal quotes* is on, pick the **Deal pipeline** and **Deal stage**; if off, **Associate with a deal** or **Create a new deal**.
  3. Choose a **quote template** (renewal/change templates), set the **Change effective date** via the date picker, optionally clear **Prorate charges and credits for the remaining billing period**, then click **Create quote**.
  4. You land in the quote editor with seller and buyer details auto-populated from the contract; adjust the *Summary* module (*Change effective date*: On agreement / Custom Date / Delayed start days / months), *Term length*, *Total discount* format, *Total contract value* → **Share**.
  5. Automated variant: `Automation > Workflows` → create/edit a deal-based workflow → set **enrollment triggers** → click **+** → **CRM** → **Create renewal quote from contract** → **Contract** dropdown (a recommended option or *Contracts: all associated*) → **Template** dropdown → **Deal selection method** = *New deal using default pipeline/stage* or *Existing deal* → **Save** → **Review and turn on**.
  6. After the buyer accepts: payment runs through the quote payment process, the **new contract is automatically associated with the previous contract**, and the renewal appears on the *Activities* and *History* tabs of both contracts.
- **data_flow:** Contract record (existing line items, term length, billing schedule, effective dates, currency) + renewal/change quote template → new quote record prefilled with contract seller/buyer data and the contract's commercial content, with a change effective date and optional proration → buyer accepts → a **new contract** is created and linked to the prior contract, forming a renewal chain, and a **deal** is created in the chosen pipeline/stage (or the workflow auto-creates one) to track the renewal opportunity.
- **data_sources:** HubSpot contract object, quote object, quote template (renewal/change), deal object, pipeline/stage configuration, billing processor, quote activity log.
- **apis_hit:** HubSpot — quote object create/update (`POST /crm/objects/{version}/quotes`) and association to the originating contract/deal; renewal quote template id attached as association type `286` at creation; contract renewal chain is surfaced on contract associations. The *Create renewal quote from contract* workflow action is a deal-based workflow action (workflows tool, not a documented REST endpoint on the pages read).
- **automations:** Deal creation can be fully automatic (setting or workflow action). The renewal **alert** appears on the contract "before the renewal date" using a configured day offset. Renewal workflows can trigger relative to *Contract > Renewal date* with an optional **Re-enroll** switch. On acceptance, contract-to-previous-contract association and payment/invoicing happen automatically. Note the workflow trigger semantics: "If a renewal hasn't been finalized, or a renewal quote isn't added to the contract, the renewal date will be the date the contract ends. If a renewal has been finalized or a renewal quote has been accepted by the customer, the renewal date will be the effective date of the renewal quote."
- **features_tools:** Contract **Actions** menu, renewal/change quote templates, quote editor *Summary* module, proration checkbox, contract **Renewals** settings with alert offsets, workflow **Create renewal quote from contract** action, deal pipeline/stage pickers, contract *Activities* and *History* tabs.
- **extensibility:** Choice of template per renewal; custom effective dates and delayed starts; proration toggle; deal auto-creation with configurable pipeline/stage (setting or workflow); direct contract renewal (beta) bypasses the quote; renewal workflow can target a single contract or *Contracts: all associated*; evergreen contracts (all line items set to *Automatically renew until canceled*) get special treatment.

receipt. 292 backend tests and 63 frontend tests. Coverage is 100 percent on the domain
package and 99 percent on the feature module. 1,626 routes swept across 88 features with
no 5xx. The seeder prints states that are not all successes. The contract guard reports
16 changed files and none shared. The whole chain was driven by hand over real HTTP
against a seeded database, and the page was rendered and measured in Chrome at four
